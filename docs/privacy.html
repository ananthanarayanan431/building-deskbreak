Privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026

This policy covers the DeskBreak app for macOS and this website. DeskBreak is made by Anantha Narayanan.

The short version

The app collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no network code, so nothing it records is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores on your Mac

DeskBreak keeps two files, both in your user account and readable only by you:

  • A reminder log at ~/Library/Application Support/DeskBreak/reminder-log.json. Each entry has the reminder type (water or walk), when it appeared, when you answered, and your answer. Entries older than 60 days are deleted automatically.
  • Your settings and the current timer state, in ~/Library/Preferences/io.github.ananthanarayanan431.deskbreak.plist.

What the app reads

To tell whether you are at your desk, DeskBreak reads how many seconds have passed since your last keyboard or mouse input, and whether the screen is locked or the Mac is asleep. It does not read what you type, which apps you use or what is on your screen. It does not ask for Accessibility, Input Monitoring, Screen Recording or Full Disk Access permission.

Reminders

Reminders are drawn by DeskBreak itself as a small window on your screen. They are created on your Mac and do not pass through any server.

Downloading the app

When you install DeskBreak with Homebrew, the source code is downloaded from GitHub. GitHub and Homebrew handle that download under their own privacy policies. We do not receive information about who installs the app.

This website

This website has no analytics, no cookies and no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser also sends your IP address to Google when a page loads, as described in the Google privacy policy. We do not see or keep any of this information.

Deleting your data

Everything DeskBreak stores is on your Mac. To remove it, quit the app and run:

rm -rf ~/Library/Application\ Support/DeskBreak
defaults delete io.github.ananthanarayanan431.deskbreak

Children

DeskBreak does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
